## 1. Project Background & Context

Imagine a high-growth global payments facilitator processing multi-million transaction event streams across North America and Europe. The business operates a multi-rail infrastructure supporting traditional card networks (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), debit networks, tokenized mobile digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL), and Account-to-Account (A2A) instant bank rails.

Executive leadership faced critical analytical visibility blind spots:
1. **Unquantified Checkout Attrition:** Over $13M in Gross Transaction Value was failing or declining at checkout annually without an actionable taxonomy separating unrecoverable hard declines from addressable soft issuer/network errors.
2. **Margin Compression:** Pass-through interchange and card network scheme fees were eroding gross merchant processing revenues (MDR), with 118 high-volume merchants operating at suboptimal net contribution margins.
3. **Operational Disruption:** Periodic regional gateway degradations and card-testing bot attacks caused unmonitored conversion drops and customer support ticket surges.
4. **Customer Retention Uncertainty:** Inability to track cohort recurrence decay and customer lifetime value beyond simple descriptive totals.

PAYMENTIQ was engineered to solve these exact enterprise problems through an end-to-end data pipeline, relational data warehouse, advanced analytical models, interactive Power BI decision suites, and grounded conversational AI.

---

## 2. Strategic Objectives & North Star Metrics

- **Primary Conversion Metric:** Maximize platform Authorization Rate (%) while maintaining dispute ratios below card network monitoring limits (<100 bps).
- **Financial Optimization Metric:** Identify and recapture addressable soft decline leakage via automated retry scheduling and dynamic routing (addressable prize: **+$103K+ net revenue**).
- **Commercial Portfolio Metric:** Classify merchants into the 4-Quadrant Opportunity Matrix to guide contract renegotiation from flat blended fees to transparent Interchange Plus (IC+) pricing.
- **Operational SLA Metric:** Deflect authorization-related customer service inquiries and reduce mean resolution time below 240 minutes.

---

## 3. Scope of Analytics & Intelligence Layers

1. **Payment Performance Intelligence:** Rail-by-rail conversion funnels, decline codes, network latency, and 3D-Secure authentication efficacy.
2. **Revenue Leakage Analytics:** Transaction Value at Risk (TVaR) modeling, recoverable soft decline capture curves, and chargeback write-off provisions.
3. **Customer Intelligence:** Quintile-based RFM behavioral segmentation, 12-month triangle cohort retention modeling, and discounted cash-flow forward CLV.
4. **Merchant Intelligence:** Portfolio economics, Herfindahl-Hirschman concentration (HHI), and 4-Quadrant strategic classification.
5. **Machine Learning & Anomaly Surveillance:** Unsupervised Isolation Forest anomaly detection for proactive threat isolation without label contamination.
6. **Time-Series Forecasting:** Holt-Winters Triple Exponential Smoothing forecasting volume and GTV 90 days forward with empirical prediction envelopes.
7. **Inferential Statistics:** Two-Sample Proportion Z-Testing proving the commercial and statistical significance of 3DS authentication.
8. **Business Intelligence:** Open-source Power BI Project (`.pbip` / TMDL) with 6 interactive pages and 30+ audited DAX measures.
9. **Grounded AI Analytics:** Natural-language conversational interface backed by AST query validation and 100% zero-hallucination verification.
