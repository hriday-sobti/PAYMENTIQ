## 1. Customer RFM Segmentation Methodology

### 1.1 Observation Window & Dimension Calculation
- **Observation Period:** 2024-01-01 00:00:00 to 2024-12-31 23:59:59 (Full Calendar Year).
- **Recency ($R$):** Elapsed days from cardholder's latest transaction timestamp to the fixed observation cutoff (`2024-12-31`):
  $$R_i = \text{DateDiff}(\max(t_i), \text{2024-12-31})$$
- **Frequency ($F$):** Total count of successfully authorized transactions in the observation window:
  $$F_i = \sum_{j=1}^{N_i} \mathbb{I}(\text{Status}_{i,j} = \text{Approved})$$
- **Monetary ($M$):** Total settled gross spend in USD:
  $$M_i = \sum_{j=1}^{N_i} \text{Amount}_{i,j} \times \mathbb{I}(\text{Status}_{i,j} = \text{Approved})$$

### 1.2 Quintile Scoring & Segment Mapping
Each cardholder is assigned an integer score $s \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ across $R$, $F$, and $M$ using empirical quintiles (`NTILE(5)` in SQL):
- **R-Score = 5** represents the lowest recency days (most recently active).
- **F-Score = 5** represents the top 20% highest transaction counts.
- **M-Score = 5** represents the top 20% highest cumulative spend.

Composite cells $R \times F \times M$ map deterministically to 7 strategic behavioral segments:
- **Champions:** $R \ge 4, F \ge 4, M \ge 4$ (highest frequency, highest spend, recent).
- **Loyal Customers:** $R \ge 3, F \ge 3, M \ge 3$ (consistent recurring volume).
- **Recent High Spenders:** $R \ge 4, F \le 2, M \ge 3$ (new high-ticket onboarding).
- **New Active Customers:** $R \ge 4, F \le 2, M \le 2$ (recent initial transactions).
- **At Risk / Churn Alert:** $R \le 2, F \ge 3, M \ge 3$ (previously high value, dormant $>120$ days).
- **Can Not Lose Them:** $R \le 2, F \ge 4, M \ge 4$ (historical VIPs showing acute churn).
- **Hibernating / Dormant:** $R \le 2, F \le 2, M \le 2$ (low volume, long inactivity).

## 2. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Methodology

### 2.1 Historical Realized Contribution
Measures actual historical retained earnings generated by customer $i$:
$$\text{Historical CLV}_i = \sum_{j=1}^{N_i} \text{Net Contribution}_{i,j}$$

### 2.2 Retention-Adjusted Forward CLV Projection
Projects expected 24-month forward discounted cash flows using empirical cohort retention:
$$\text{Projected CLV}_i = \sum_{t=1}^{24} \frac{\bar{m}_i \cdot r^t}{(1 + d)^t}$$
Where:
- $\bar{m}_i$ is the average monthly net contribution for the customer's segment.
- $r$ is the empirical cohort monthly retention parameter ($r \approx 0.88$ for stable cohorts).
- $d$ is the monthly cost of capital discount rate ($d = 0.01$, equivalent to 12.7% annual WACC).

## 3. Unsupervised Anomaly Detection Methodology

### 3.1 Model Architecture: Isolation Forest
Isolation Forest isolates anomalies by randomly selecting a feature and randomly splitting value bounds. Because anomalies require fewer recursive splits to isolate, they exhibit shorter path lengths in the ensemble trees.

- **Algorithm:** Scikit-Learn `IsolationForest`
- **Ensemble Size:** 100 decision trees (`n_estimators=100`).
- **Contamination Parameter:** 0.018 (calibrated to baseline payment anomaly prevalence of ~1.8%).
- **Feature Vector:** Normalized standard scaler applied to:
  $$\mathbf{x} = [\text{Amount}, \text{Latency}, \text{Risk Score}, \mathbb{I}_{\text{Cross-Border}}, \mathbb{I}_{\text{High-Value}}, \mathbb{I}_{\text{3DS}}]$$
- **Anomaly Score Formulation:**
  $$s(x, n) = 2^{-\frac{E(h(x))}{c(n)}}$$
  Where $h(x)$ is the path length of observation $x$, $E(h(x))$ is the expected path length across the forest, and $c(n)$ is the average path length of unsuccessful searches in a Binary Search Tree.

## 4. Time-Series Forecasting Methodology

### 4.1 Model Formulation: Holt-Winters Triple Exponential Smoothing
Daily transaction volume $y_t$ and settled GTV exhibit linear growth trends and strong weekly 7-day diurnal cycles:

$$\hat{y}_{t+h|t} = \ell_t + h b_t + s_{t+h-m(k+1)}$$

- **Level Equation:** $\ell_t = \alpha (y_t - s_{t-m}) + (1 - \alpha)(\ell_{t-1} + b_{t-1})$
- **Trend Equation:** $b_t = \beta (\ell_t - \ell_{t-1}) + (1 - \beta) b_{t-1}$
- **Seasonal Equation:** $s_t = \gamma (y_t - \ell_{t-1} - b_{t-1}) + (1 - \gamma) s_{t-m}$
- **Seasonality Period:** $m = 7$ days.
- **Optimization Criterion:** L-BFGS-B minimizing Sum of Squared Errors (SSE).

### 4.2 Error Evaluation Metrics
Out-of-sample backtest validation splits data into chronological 80% train / 20% test:
$$\text{MAE} = \frac{1}{n} \sum |y_t - \hat{y}_t|$$
$$\text{RMSE} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n} \sum (y_t - \hat{y}_t)^2}$$
$$\text{MAPE} = \frac{100\%}{n} \sum \left| \frac{y_t - \hat{y}_t}{y_t} \right|$$

## 5. Inferential Statistical Hypothesis Testing Methodology

### 5.1 Two-Sample Pooled Proportion Z-Test
Evaluates whether 3D-Secure verification produces a statistically significant lift in E-Commerce Card-Not-Present authorization rates:
$$H_0: p_1 - p_2 = 0 \quad \text{vs.} \quad H_1: p_1 - p_2 \ne 0$$

- **Pooled Sample Proportion:**
  $$\hat{p} = \frac{x_1 + x_2}{n_1 + n_2}$$
- **Pooled Standard Error:**
  $$SE_{pool} = \sqrt{\hat{p}(1 - \hat{p}) \left( \frac{1}{n_1} + \frac{1}{n_2} \right)}$$
- **Test Statistic:**
  $$Z = \frac{\hat{p}_1 - \hat{p}_2}{SE_{pool}}$$

### 5.2 Standardized Effect Size: Cohen's $h$
Calculates the magnitude of divergence independent of sample size:
$$h = 2 \cdot \left( \arcsin\sqrt{\hat{p}_1} - \arcsin\sqrt{\hat{p}_2} \right)$$
- $h \approx 0.20$: Small operational effect.
- $h \approx 0.50$: Moderate operational effect.
- $h \approx 0.80$: Large operational effect.
